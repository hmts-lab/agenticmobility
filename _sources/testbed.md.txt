# The testbed: a simplified Montreal

Experiments are run on one network, in the way much of transport research uses Sioux Falls (24 nodes): small
enough to be read off a map, fixed, and the same for everybody. The testbed is the scenario `montreal`.

```{raw} html

```

## The network

25 nodes and 51 two-way roads, about 21 km by 17 km (`agmsim/montreal.py`, `network: {type: montreal}`). It
keeps what shapes traffic in the city:

- **an island** reached over a handful of crossings: the Champlain, Victoria and Jacques-Cartier bridges and
  the Lafontaine tunnel to the South Shore, four bridges to Laval;
- **a centre on its south side**: Downtown, McGill, Old Montreal, the Quartier Latin;
- **a ring of expressways**: the A-40 Metropolitan, the A-15 Decarie, the A-20, the Ville-Marie and the A-25;
- **the arterials between them**: Sherbrooke, Saint-Denis, Papineau, Pie-IX, Henri-Bourassa and others.

| Class of road | Roads | Free-flow speed | Lanes each way | Lets out, per lane and hour |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| expressway | 15 | 80 km/h | 3 | 2,000 vehicles |
| bridge or tunnel | 8 | 60 to 70 km/h | 2 or 3 | 1,600 to 1,900 |
| arterial | 28 | 36 km/h | 2 | 850 |

An arterial here is several kilometres of street with lights along the way: its free speed and its capacity
are what they leave of a 50 km/h limit and of a lane's saturation flow.

Positions are approximate (read off a map by district), lengths are straight-line distances times 1.2,
speeds, lanes and capacities are typical values for the class of road. **Road by road it is an abstraction
to experiment on, not a model of the city.** The morning as a whole is tuned to survey numbers, see below.

## The morning

`agmsim run montreal` simulates a weekday from 06:00 to 12:00 with everything the simulator has:

| Part | In the testbed |
| - | - |
| Travellers | 900, living where people live (weights by district), 70% with a car; most set out around 08:00 |
| Places | 21 named destinations: downtown offices, the Technoparc, YUL airport, McGill, UdeM, UQAM, three hospitals, markets and malls, the Bell Centre, the Olympic Park |
| Private cars | one per car-owning traveller, electric, level L1 (re-route on traffic) |
| On-demand cars | a fleet of 12 at a taxi's tariff, level L1 |
| Transit | four lines on their own right of way, drawn as loops: Orange, Green, South, West; ordinary buses everywhere, as timed trips |
| Traffic lights | at 10 junctions of the inner city, deciding by their own queues (L1) |
| Charging stations | 7 stations, 17 chargers, dynamic price (L1) |
| Orchestrator | publishes advisories (L1); a cordon around Downtown that can carry a charge |
| Event | a crash on the Decarie expressway, 07:30 to 08:10, traffic at 30% of its speed |

With these settings and seed 1 the mean trip takes 29 minutes, 72% of trips are made by car, 26% by transit
and 2% by on-demand car; nine trips that start last are still under way at noon. A run with the built-in
decision models takes about 15 seconds.

## The size of the morning

The morning is tuned so that it comes out at the size surveys give it. `agmsim check` holds a run against
these numbers, next to its checks of consistency:

| Measure | Survey | Accepted | The testbed, seed 1 |
| - | - | - | - |
| a trip, all ways of travelling | 29.0 min ([Statistics Canada, May 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/250826/dq250826a-eng.htm)) | 24 to 34 min | 29.0 min |
| trips by car | 70% of commuters (Statistics Canada) | 62% to 78% | 72.2% |
| trips by transit | 22% of trips not on foot, by bike or school bus ([ARTM 2023](https://www.artm.quebec/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/ARTM_Enquete2023_Faits_Saillants.pdf)) | 15% to 30% | 25.7% |
| trips by on-demand car | 1% (ARTM 2023: other, taxi included) | at most 3% | 1.8% |
| trips that start before 09:00 | 60% (ARTM 2023) | 50% to 70% | 67.0% |
| a car trip, door to door | 25.5 km/h ([TomTom 2025](https://www.tomtom.com/traffic-index/city/montreal/), city average) | 18 to 35 km/h | 28.1 km/h |

The simulated trips are those of adults over at least 3 km, so the survey's trips on foot, by bike and by
school bus have no counterpart. 900 travellers stand for the whole region: one simulated car is a platoon of
some 250 real ones, so these sizes hold for the morning as a whole, and a single road is coarse.

## Changing a condition

An experiment changes settings of the testbed and leaves everything else as it is:

```bash
agmsim run montreal --set agents.vehicle.agency=L0          # cars that follow their first route
agmsim run montreal --set agents.operator.initial_toll=2    # a charge of 2 USD to enter the centre
agmsim run montreal --set events=[]                         # the same morning without the crash
agmsim run montreal --set agents.traveler.count=1500        # more people
```

Every setting is described in [](configuration.md).
